# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, engineering managers, collaborators, and curious peers visit this portfolio to understand Vardan Vanyan's work, craft, and technical range quickly. They may arrive from a resume, GitHub, LinkedIn, or direct portfolio link, often with limited time and a need to verify both taste and implementation ability.

## Product Purpose

This site showcases Vardan's projects and front-end engineering sensibility through an interactive desktop-inspired portfolio. Success means visitors can find projects, resume, contact paths, and technical context without friction while the interface itself demonstrates polish, systems thinking, and care.

## Brand Personality

Polished, inventive, and technically fluent. The tone should feel capable and playful without becoming gimmicky, with enough restraint that the work remains easier to inspect than the interface is to admire.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic resume templates, static one-page portfolio sameness, SaaS-style hero metrics, novelty desktop effects that slow down basic navigation, and visuals that obscure project evidence. The OS metaphor should support discovery rather than compete with it.

## Design Principles

Make interaction the portfolio signal.

Keep the important paths obvious: projects, resume, contact, GitHub, and LinkedIn should never feel hidden behind the metaphor.

Performance is part of craft; the desktop should feel light, responsive, and respectful on mobile and slower hardware.

Prefer evidence over ornament: screenshots, project context, and working links should carry more weight than decorative effects.

Preserve accessibility defaults, including keyboard navigation, reduced motion support, readable contrast, and clear focus states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA for contrast, focus visibility, keyboard access, semantic structure, and motion sensitivity. Reduced-motion users should receive a calmer experience without losing access to content or controls.
